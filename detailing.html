Premier yacht detailing and maintenance.

Knight Marine serves marinas across the South Coast and Solent—from Lymington to Chichester, including the Isle of Wight. We provide comprehensive yacht care designed to keep your vessel in outstanding condition all year round.

From routine washdowns to professional machine compound cutting and polishing of hulls and topsides, our experienced team helps maintain your yacht’s appearance, protection and long-term value.

Professional deck and hull care.

We recommend polishing your yacht once or twice a year to maintain a deep gloss finish and protect the gelcoat from weathering, oxidation and general wear. Regular deck and hull polishing not only keeps your boat looking exceptional, it also helps preserve its condition for years to come.

Using specialist marine polishing compounds and detailing products, we can:

  • Remove stains, oxidation and ingrained dirt
  • Restore faded or chalky GRP surfaces
  • Revive hulls and topsides to a high-gloss finish
  • Protect surfaces against future deterioration
  • Maintain the appearance and value of your vessel

Trusted detailing specialists.

Knight Marine has built a strong reputation for reliability, quality workmanship and exceptional customer care. We work on vessels of all sizes—from luxury yachts to day boats—delivering meticulous attention to detail on every project.

Alongside detailing and polishing, we can advise on GRP repairs and restoration work, liaise with trusted local marine contractors, arrange replacement decals, stickers and hull stripes, and provide tailored yacht-care and maintenance packages.

Personalised yacht care packages.

Every vessel is different, which is why we offer bespoke care packages tailored to you. Whether you need regular maintenance, seasonal polishing or a complete detailing programme, we provide a dependable one-stop service to keep your boat looking its best.
