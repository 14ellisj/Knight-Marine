Yacht Detailing & Maintenance at Universal Marina

Knight Marine is a premier yacht detailing and maintenance provider, serving marinas across the South Coast and Solent — from Lymington to Chichester, including the Isle of Wight.

We provide comprehensive yacht care services designed to keep your vessel in outstanding condition all year round. From routine washdowns to professional machine compound cutting and polishing of hulls and topsides, our experienced team helps maintain your yacht’s appearance, protection, and long-term value.

Professional Deck & Hull Care

We recommend polishing your yacht once or twice a year to maintain a deep gloss finish and protect the gelcoat from weathering, oxidation, and general wear. Regular deck and hull polishing not only keeps your boat looking exceptional but also helps preserve its condition for years to come.

Using specialist marine polishing compounds and detailing products, we can:

  • Remove stains, oxidation, and ingrained dirt
  • Restore faded or chalky GRP surfaces
  • Revive hulls and topsides to a high-gloss finish
  • Protect surfaces against future deterioration
  • Maintain the appearance and value of your vessel
