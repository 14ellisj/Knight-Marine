Common symptoms

When to ask for a system check.

These symptoms can point to a component fault, poor configuration, a connection problem or a wider system-design issue.

Batteries keep going flat

We assess battery condition, charging sources, standing loads and whether capacity suits the boat.

Shore power is not charging

We check supply, protection, chargers, connections and the configuration between AC and DC systems.

Alternator charging is poor

We investigate alternator output, regulation, cabling, battery acceptance and DC-DC charging.

Electronics switch off

Voltage drop, weak connections, undersized cabling or distribution faults can interrupt sensitive equipment.

Monitor readings look wrong

Battery monitors depend on correct wiring, configuration, synchronisation and complete load measurement.

Network faults are intermittent

We inspect power, termination, connectors, sensors and NMEA data flow before replacing equipment.
