Fogstar marine lithium installations on the South Coast

Knight Marine Services is listed in Fogstar’s Installer/Retailer Locator as an Installer (Fogstar Drift – Marine) at Unit 5, Universal Marina. Speak to our team about a Fogstar battery installation or a wider marine lithium upgrade on the River Hamble.

Plan the battery upgrade around the whole boat

Tell us what you run aboard, how long you spend away from shore power and what charging equipment you already have. We assess battery space and access, the existing charging arrangements, monitoring and the loads you want to support before recommending a system.

Send photographs and model numbers for your batteries, shore charger, alternator charging equipment, solar controller and inverter. This helps us identify the work needed around the battery installation.

Fogstar batteries and Victron power systems

A lithium project may include changes to charging, monitoring and inverter equipment. We can assess Fogstar batteries alongside Victron marine power systems, checking the exact models and installation requirements before specifying how they will work together.

The quotation should cover the agreed battery, charging, protection, cabling, monitoring and commissioning work so you can understand the scope of the upgrade.

Can I replace my existing batteries with lithium?

The answer depends on the current installation. A battery change can affect how the boat charges and how connected equipment behaves. We review the system first and explain which components can be retained and which need attention.

Can you check an existing lithium installation?

Yes. Tell us about charging problems, battery warnings or inconsistent monitor readings, and include the battery and charger models. Our marine electrician service covers fault finding and planned upgrades from Universal Marina.

What should I send for a marine lithium quotation?

Include the boat make and model, where it is berthed, the existing battery bank and charging equipment, and what you want to power. Let us know if the work needs to fit around a lift-out, refit or planned departure.
