Integrated marine power specialists.

Knight Marine delivers high-performance marine electrical systems for yachts and powerboats on the River Hamble, Southampton, Portsmouth and across the South Coast.

We specialise in lithium battery systems, marine solar panels, inverter chargers, battery management systems and complete off-grid power solutions designed for life on the water.

More comfort. Less generator noise.

Our bespoke marine electrical installations can help you run everything from kettles and coffee machines to refrigeration and full onboard air conditioning-all in silence, without relying on a noisy generator. Whether you own a sailing yacht, motor yacht, catamaran or powerboat, we tailor an upgrade to your vessel and cruising lifestyle.

  • Integrated inverter and charging systems
  • Lithium and AGM battery solutions
  • Marine solar panel installations
  • Cerbo GX and VRM remote monitoring
  • Battery management and alternator charging
  • Shore power upgrades and electrical refits
  • Electrical fault finding and system health checks
