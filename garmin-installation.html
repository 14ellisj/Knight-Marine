Garmin marine electronics installation on the Hamble

Plan a Garmin installation with Knight Marine at Universal Marina. We install and integrate navigation equipment for yachts and motorboats, from replacing a helm display to planning a connected chartplotter, radar, AIS and instrument system.

Start with the equipment already aboard and how you use your boat. We assess the display positions, power supply, cable routes and network connections before recommending the scope of the installation.

Garmin chartplotter installation

A new chartplotter needs to suit the helm, your cruising and the equipment you want to connect. Send the model of your current display, photographs of the console and the devices you want to retain. We can discuss mounting, connections and the work involved in replacing or extending your system.

Garmin radar installation

We review the radar and chartplotter combination, mounting position and cable route together. Compatibility and available radar features depend on the exact models. Garmin radar uses its marine network connection, so an existing NMEA 2000 backbone alone does not provide the radar connection.

Garmin’s radar installation and compatibility guidance

Garmin AIS installation and integration

Tell us whether you need to receive AIS targets, transmit your vessel’s position, or investigate missing targets on a display. We assess the AIS equipment, antenna arrangement and connection to your chartplotter. The required network connection depends on the equipment models.

Garmin instruments and commissioning

Wind, speed and depth instruments need suitable sensors, a working network and configuration that matches the boat. We can plan instrument replacements alongside the helm display and review which existing sensors can be retained.

Discuss calibration and sea-trial commissioning as part of the installation, particularly for sailing instruments and autopilot work.

Can I upgrade my Garmin system in stages?

Yes, where the equipment and installation allow it. Send a list of the current displays, radar, AIS, sensors and network components so we can assess what works together and plan the order of upgrades.

Can you investigate an existing installation?

Tell us which equipment is affected, what the screen shows and when the fault occurs. We offer marine electrical fault finding and specialist marine electronics support, including power and network issues that can affect navigation equipment.
