Plan your winter maintenance

If your boat is coming ashore on the River Hamble, the lift-out is a good time to inspect the anodes, seacocks and running gear, prepare the hull for antifouling and tackle any polishing before launch.

Staying afloat this winter? Tell us about any charging faults, battery concerns or servicing you have in mind. We can help you decide what to do alongside and what to leave until your next lift-out.

Include your boat’s make and model, where she’s berthed and any lift-out or launch dates you already have.

Maintenance services

Yacht servicing and repairs

Our marina-based team handles routine care, annual servicing and specialist repair work for yachts, motorboats and leisure craft across the Hamble, Solent and South Coast.

01

Rudder bearing servicing

Annual inspection of bearings, linkages, wires and steering mechanisms, with bearing replacement, adjustment and lubrication where required.

Cutlass bearing servicing

Inspection and replacement of worn water-lubricated bearings to help prevent vibration and damage to shafts, P-brackets and gearboxes.

Ask about cutlass bearings 03

Propeller & shaft cleaning

Careful removal of marine growth, condition checks, polishing or painting, plus support with damaged propeller repair or replacement.

Ask about running gear 04

Teak repair & protection

Cleaning, restoration and protective treatments that reduce discolouration, limescale, algae growth and seasonal deterioration.

Ask about teak care 05

Anode replacement

Inspection and replacement of correctly specified sacrificial anodes to protect underwater metalwork from electrolytic corrosion.

Ask about anodes 06

Seacock replacement

Checks, servicing and replacement of worn seacocks and through-hull fittings using appropriate materials, bonding and secure hose connections.

Ask about seacocks 07

Mast servicing & repair

Inspection and maintenance for mast fittings, rigging components and associated equipment to identify wear before it becomes a problem.

Deck & hull polishing

Professional washdowns, compound cutting, polishing and GRP restoration to protect gelcoat and revive faded or chalky surfaces.

Antifouling
