Protect performance, efficiency and hull condition.

Knight Marine provides professional yacht antifouling services from our base at Universal Marina, helping boat owners protect performance, efficiency and hull condition throughout the season.

Regular antifouling is essential to maintain your yacht’s performance and protect the hull from marine growth, fouling and long-term damage. A properly maintained hull supports fuel efficiency, handling and overall vessel performance.

Professional yacht antifouling services.

Our experienced team works on vessels of all sizes, delivering reliable antifouling preparation and application services tailored to your boat’s specific requirements. We use high-quality marine products and proven preparation techniques to achieve a durable, long-lasting finish.

  • Pressure washing and hull cleaning
  • Hull preparation and sanding
  • Antifouling paint application
  • Removal of old antifoul coatings
  • Waterline and hull inspection
  • Propeller and running gear cleaning
  • Seasonal maintenance packages
