Why install Victron Energy?

Victron Energy systems transform the way your boat generates, stores and uses power. With lithium batteries, inverter chargers, solar charging and intelligent monitoring, you can enjoy silent onboard power without constantly running a generator.

  • Run 240V appliances including kettles, coffee machines, induction hobs and air conditioning
  • Enjoy silent off-grid power without noisy generators
  • Charge batteries faster and extend battery life
  • Upgrade to lightweight lithium for more usable power
  • Keep batteries charged while at anchor with solar integration
  • Use Victron Cerbo GX and VRM Portal for remote monitoring and control
  • Reduce fuel use with more efficient energy management

Why install modern marine electronics?

Garmin and other modern marine systems improve navigation, safety and onboard integration. Modern chartplotters, radar and sonar give you clear situational awareness and control while cruising, fishing or passagemaking.

  • Advanced GPS chartplotters and touchscreen navigation
  • Radar for safer navigation in poor visibility
  • Autopilot integration for easier long-distance cruising
  • Fishfinders and sonar systems for anglers
  • Engine data and vessel monitoring in one display
  • Integration with Victron systems and NMEA 2000 networks
  • Control lighting, cameras, audio and onboard systems from one screen

The advantage of integration.

When Victron Energy and Garmin, Raymarine, B&G or Simrad systems are integrated, your boat becomes a fully connected smart vessel. You can monitor battery levels, tank levels, charging systems, solar input and power consumption directly from your display.

This means better energy awareness while cruising, easier fault monitoring and diagnostics, improved safety offshore, simpler control of onboard systems, increased resale value and a more refined boating experience.
