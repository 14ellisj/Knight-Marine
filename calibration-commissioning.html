Your electronics aren’t finished when they’re installed.

Setup that continues beyond the dock

Installation is only part of the job.

Accurate heading, wind and boat-speed data depend on sensor position, network setup, configuration and real-world calibration. Autopilots also need commissioning and tuning for the boat rather than relying only on default values.

Knight Marine provides calibration and commissioning from the River Hamble for boats in Southampton and across the Solent. The aim is practical: consistent data, predictable steering and a system the owner understands.

What gets checked

A joined-up commissioning process.

Calibration works best when the sensors, network, displays and steering system are treated as one connected installation.

STEP ONE

Inspect

Review the installed equipment, sensor locations, software, network and available data.

STEP TWO

Configure

Set vessel details, data sources, units, damping and the correct system relationships.

STEP THREE

Calibrate

Carry out the required compass, wind, speed, sensor and autopilot procedures.

STEP FOUR

Sea trial

Verify behaviour on the water, refine settings and explain the final setup.

Typical reasons to commission

When the numbers or steering do not feel right.

01

Wind data changes between tacks.

Sensor alignment, boat-speed input, compass data and calibration can all affect true-wind calculations.

02

The autopilot wanders or over-corrects.

Setup, compass calibration, steering response and sea-state parameters need to suit the vessel.

03

Heading differs between displays.

We check data sources, variation settings, sensor alignment and network configuration.

04

A new electronics system needs handover.

We can verify integration, commission the equipment and explain the working setup clearly.
