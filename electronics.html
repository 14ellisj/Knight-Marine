Marine electronics specialists on the River Hamble.

Based at Universal Marina on the River Hamble, Knight Marine provides professional marine electronics installation, upgrades and support for yachts, motorboats and leisure craft. We work with leading marine technology brands including Garmin, Raymarine and B&G to deliver reliable onboard systems tailored to your vessel and cruising needs.

Bring your boat to us at Universal Marina and let our specialist technicians help transform your onboard experience. From a single chartplotter to a fully integrated helm, we keep the advice practical and the finish professional.

Modern systems, properly integrated.

Whether you are refitting a sailing yacht, upgrading a powerboat or fitting out a new vessel, our experienced technicians provide expert advice, professional installation and ongoing technical support. From troubleshooting and repairs to complete marine electronics packages, we ensure your onboard systems are modern, reliable and easy to use.
