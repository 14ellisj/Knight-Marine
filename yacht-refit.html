Turn your next boat project into a better boating experience.

Refit support built around your boat.

A successful refit starts with a clear plan: what needs attention, what will make the biggest difference and how the work fits around your season. Knight Marine helps owners bring that plan to life with knowledgeable advice, professional installation and a trusted network of specialist support where required.

Whether you are improving a single onboard system or planning a broader refresh, we keep the project organised and focused on the way you want to use your boat.

Where we can help.

  • Navigation, communications and helm upgrades
  • Victron Energy, lithium, solar and shore-power systems
  • Audio, onboard WiFi and entertainment upgrades
  • Electrical fault finding, rewiring and system tidying
  • Seasonal maintenance, hull care and detailing
  • Practical support with trusted specialist marine contractors

One clear point of contact.

Refit work is at its best when the details are considered together. We can help you prioritise the work, make sensible equipment choices and ensure that new systems integrate naturally with what is already aboard. The result is a boat that feels better to own, use and maintain.

Based at Universal Marina on the River Hamble, we are well placed to support projects for boat owners across Southampton, Hamble, Portsmouth, the Solent and the wider South Coast.
